## English 🇬🇧

**App name:** ACE Athlete
**Subtitle** (iOS, ≤30): `Tennis athletic training`
**Short description** (Play, ≤80): `Personalized off-court athletic training for tennis, backed by science.`

**Description:**
```
ACE Athlete is your personal athletic coach for tennis — for everything off the
court that decides your game.

A short performance test (8 stations) shows exactly where you stand and rates
you fairly. From there the app builds your plan — matched to your level, your
goals and your equipment (gym, home, or none at all).

• Performance test with real norms by age & sex
• Endless level system (L1–L4) with automatic level-ups
• Personalized weekly plans & a guided workout player
• 85+ exercises with instructions, form tips and home/no-kit variants
• RAMP warm-up & prehab against the most common tennis injuries
• Monthly test with radar, trend lines and PR markers
• Cloud sync across all your devices

Train speed, agility, jumping power, core and conditioning — the things that
truly make you faster, more explosive and injury-free on court.

ACE Pro unlocks monthly tests, the full exercise library and periodized plans
up to L4.
```

**Keywords** (iOS, ≤100): `tennis,athletic,training,fitness,strength,speed,agility,prehab,workout,court,power`

**What's new** (1.0.0): `First release: performance test, personalized plans, 85+ exercises, monthly test & progress tracking.`
